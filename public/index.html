A CAMPUS-FIRST IDEA

a better way to get food across campus.

NovaDash brings ordering and delivery together in one iPhone app. Students can request food from familiar campus spots, and nearby students can choose to take the delivery.

HOW ORDERING WORKS

hungry? stay put.

01 / PICK

pick a place.

Choose from COVA, Cascia, Connelly, St. Mary’s, Wawa, or Holy Grounds.

02 / ORDER

tell us what you want.

Add your order number, describe the food, and say where to meet. You’re only charged once a student accepts.

03 / RECEIVE

meet your dasher.

When your food arrives, tap phones with your dasher or show your QR code to confirm the handoff.

HOW DASHING WORKS

already walking? get paid.

01 / BROWSE

see who’s hungry.

Open requests show the pickup spot, the drop-off, and what you’ll earn.

02 / ACCEPT

swipe to take it.

Pick one that fits your route and swipe up to accept the delivery.

03 / GET PAID

hand it off.

Scan the orderer’s QR code or tap phones, and your payout lands in your balance.
